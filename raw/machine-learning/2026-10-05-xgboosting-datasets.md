# Datasets | XGBoosting

> Source: https://xgboosting.com/datasets/
> Collected: 2026-10-05
> Published: Unknown

Helpful examples of applying XGBoost models to real-world datasets.

These are “toy” datasets typically or historically used in academia for comparing or evaluating machine learning models.

The catalog links examples for the Abalone Age, Adult, Boston Housing, California Housing, Cleveland Heart Disease, Covertype, Diabetes, Glass Identification, Handwritten Digits, Higgs Boson, Horse Colic, Ionosphere, Iris, KDDCup99, Linnerud, Pima Indians Diabetes, Sonar, Wheat Seeds, Wholesale Customers, Wine, and Wisconsin Breast Cancer datasets.

It also includes Kaggle examples for Bank Churn, Credit Card Fraud Detection, Higgs Boson, House Prices, House Sales in King County, MNIST Handwritten Digit Recognizer, Otto Group Product Classification, and Titanic.

The site describes the page as a dataset index for XGBoost examples. The linked examples, rather than this index page, contain the individual preprocessing and modeling details.
