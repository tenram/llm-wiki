Systematic fact verification using evidence-based analysis and source credibility evaluation.

The listed process is to identify the claim, determine required evidence, evaluate available evidence, rate the claim, and provide context. The six ratings are TRUE, MOSTLY TRUE, MIXED, MOSTLY FALSE, FALSE, and UNVERIFIABLE.

The source-quality hierarchy ranks peer-reviewed studies, official government statistics, reputable news organizations, qualified expert statements, general news sites, and social media or blogs. It flags statistical manipulation, context removal, false equivalences, and logical fallacies.
