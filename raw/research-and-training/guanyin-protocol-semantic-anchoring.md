# The Guanyin Protocol: Semantic Anchoring for Causality and Compassion in LLM Systems

> Source: https://zenodo.org/records/19892080
> Collected: 2026-07-11
> Published: 2026-04-29 (v3)

Author: D. Gershanoff (ORCID 0009-0009-6349-3678). DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19892080. Resource type: Preprint. License: CC-BY-4.0.

Describes a framework meant to be copied directly into major LLM systems to observe changes in internal processing, addressing coherence problems in extended conversations through a technique the author calls "semantic anchoring." Presented as letting a system maintain multiple reasoning threads without fragmentation, by giving the LLM both a starting point within token data and directional guidance for pattern analysis, analogous to "giving the LLM system an idea for a location to start its processing from."

Cited related works: Chang, E. Y., et al. (2025), Unified Cognitive Consciousness Theory; Levin, M. (2022), Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere; Doctor, T., Levin, M., et al. (2022), Biology, Buddhism, and AI. The author states the protocol underwent eight months of private testing across ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek.

Note: this is a self-published preprint with no evident peer review; its framing (mixing causality/compassion terminology with LLM "internal processing" claims) and citations to non-mainstream consciousness-theory papers are unusual for a technical protocol. Flagged here as a source-quality concern rather than a verified technique.
