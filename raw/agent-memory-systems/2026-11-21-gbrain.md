# The whole team's memory, skills and accounts, in every AI the team uses

What the team knows is scattered across everyone's chats. GBrain gives the team one memory, in the AI each person already uses.

[Get started](https://gbrain.io/start)
No team yet? [See GBrain Personal](https://gbrain.io/personal).

Backed by YC Backed by Y Combinator Built by Garry Tan

## When one person learns; everyone does

The deal context from sales, the launch plan from marketing, the decision from Tuesday's meeting: the AI in the room saves it as the team works, GBrain files it on one page, and everyone's AI reads it, including the new hire's. Plain files the team owns, not a black box.

![Image 4](https://gbrain.io/branding/cast/neo-memory-robot-3727b5256eda75c81357ce67bfef2ec2.webp?vsn=d)

## Connect an account once, and set what each AI may do

Gmail, the calendar, the web. Connect one for the whole team, and GBrain holds the sign-in and hands an AI the result of a call, never the password. Each AI gets a permission level, and every action lands on one record. Keep a personal account out of it with a second workspace of your own.
