# Town: AI Assistant Product Overview

> Source: https://www.town.com/
> Collected: 2026-07-21
> Published: Unknown

## What It Is
Town is a personal AI assistant platform that automates routine work tasks. Users are paired with a customizable "Townie" character that learns their work patterns and handles recurring tasks independently.

## Core Purpose
The platform aims to eliminate tedious administrative work by having AI assistants manage inbox organization, meeting scheduling, task completion, and information synthesis—allowing users to focus on higher-value work.

## Key Features

**Learning & Personalization**
- Townies learn user work patterns on day one without requiring manual training
- Adapt to individual communication styles and client preferences

**Automation Capabilities**
- **To-Do List Management**: Process task lists and provide status updates
- **Routines**: Handle recurring work (email management, meeting prep, competitive research, relationship tracking)
- **Suggestions**: Proactively identify optimization opportunities

**Team Integration**
- Townies can work across team platforms and tools
- Support for shared workflows and processes

**Integrations**
Over 50+ integrations including Gmail, Google Workspace, Slack, HubSpot, Notion, Linear, Salesforce, and numerous other business tools.

## Target Audience
- Busy professionals (investors, sales leaders, talent managers)
- Teams and organizations seeking workflow automation
- Users wanting AI assistance without extensive prompt engineering

## Privacy & Trust
The platform emphasizes data privacy, encryption, SOC II Type 2 compliance, and no model training on user data unless explicitly opted-in.
