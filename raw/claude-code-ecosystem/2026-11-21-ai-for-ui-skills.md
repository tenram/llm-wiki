## Available skills 19

Ordered in the way you’d reach for them in an actual project.

01

### Before you build

Learn the right words, pick what to build it with, and compare real options before deciding.

*   [Animation Vocabulary The exact word you need to describe what you want.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/animation-vocabulary)
*   [Pick a UI Library Your agent can choose a library from a curated list of options.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/pick-ui-library)
*   [Prototyping Build several variants before settling on one.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/prototype)
*   [Ask Emil Points you to the right skill for the job.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/ask-emil)

02

### Building the interface

The skills that will help you make the right decisions for your interface.

*   [Design Engineering The main skill. Contains my design engineering thinking.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/emil-design-eng)
*   [Animate Builds animation for you, with the right ingredients.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/animate)
*   [Choreograph Coordinate several elements that move together.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/choreograph)
*   [Apple Design Fluid, physical motion, the way Apple would build it.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/apple-design)
*   [Gestures Drag, swipe, and sheets that feel like they are supposed to.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/gestures)
*   [Mobile Native The small fixes that make a web app feel great on a phone.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/mobile-native)

03

### Refining and reviewing

Go an extra mile, ensure everything is performant, and that the motion feels right.

*   [Animation Performance Ensure your app is fast and animates at 60 frames per second.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/animation-performance)
*   [Break UI Test your UI with long names, unusual emails, long lists, etc.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/break-ui)
*   [Review Animations A strict review that results in an approval or a block decision.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/review-animations)

04

### Looking at the whole app

When not building, zoom out. Improve the experience of the whole app.

*   [Find Opportunities See where motion in your app makes sense, and where it doesn’t.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/find-animation-opportunities)
*   [Improve Animations An audit of all your motion, with plans for subagents to execute.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/improve-animations)
*   [Remove Animations Removes unnecessary animations to improve the experience.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/remove-animations)

05

### Other skills

Ranging from specific tools and platforms to more general skills.

*   [Animate Expo Build better animations in Expo, built in collab with the Expo team.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/animate-expo)
*   [Ask Sonner Setup, recipes, and fixes for my toast library.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/ask-sonner)
*   [Learn Taste Turns your corrections into rules the next agent follows.](https://aiforui.dev/skills/learn-taste)
