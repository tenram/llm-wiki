# ZeroTier Pricing and Platform Overview

> Source: https://www.zerotier.com/pricing/
> Collected: 2026-07-21
> Published: Unknown

## What is ZeroTier?

ZeroTier is a networking platform that enables secure connectivity across devices and networks. The service positions itself as "next-generation connectivity and cybersecurity," designed to scale from personal use through enterprise deployments.

## Pricing Tiers

ZeroTier offers five distinct plans:

**Personal (Free)**
- 10 devices, 1 network
- Perpetual free access
- Basic features including AES-256 encryption

**Essential ($18/month)**
- 10 devices included, $2 per additional device
- Up to 10 networks
- OIDC support and basic admin capabilities

**Scale ($179/month)**
- 100 devices included, $1.80 per additional device
- Unlimited networks
- Enhanced features for service providers

**Enterprise (Custom pricing)**
- Designed for organizations with 500+ devices
- Custom compliance options including self-hosted deployments
- Priority support and extensive feature set

**Quantum (Custom pricing)**
- Highest security tier for mission-critical infrastructure
- Sovereign deployment options
- ML-KEM-1024 encryption and advanced cryptographic features

## Key Features by Tier

Features progress across tiers, including:
- Network administration capabilities
- Premium relay support
- Access control and policy enforcement
- API, CLI, and UI interfaces
- Compliance certifications (SOC 2, GDPR, ISO 27001, FIPS 140-3)
- Advanced encryption and security protocols

## Target Use Cases

- **Personal**: Hobbyists, smart home enthusiasts, gamers
- **Essential**: Small to medium businesses with VPN or SD-WAN needs
- **Scale**: Large deployments, service providers, complex networks
- **Enterprise**: Organizations with strict compliance requirements
- **Quantum**: Defense, government, and mission-critical sectors

The platform emphasizes infinite scalability and flexible pricing for MSPs, educational institutions, and charities (up to 50% discount available).
