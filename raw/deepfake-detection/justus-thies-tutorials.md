# Tutorials & Demos (Justus Thies)

> Source: https://justusthies.github.io/tutorials/
> Collected: 2026-07-11
> Published: Unknown (page spans 2016-2023)

Hosted by Prof. Justus Thies (TU Darmstadt), catalogs tutorials and demos from 2016 through 2023 spanning facial capture, neural rendering, synthetic media, and media forensics.

Notable entries by year:
- 2023: "Cyber and the City," an exhibition at Schloss Museum, Tübingen, on facial reenactment and detecting manipulated media.
- 2021: a 3DV tutorial on neural rendering advances; an expert interview for the European Parliamentary Research Service on deepfakes; a SIGGRAPH course on neural rendering.
- 2020: a CVPR neural rendering tutorial; a CVPR media forensics workshop; exhibitions on AI and intellectual property.
- 2018-2019: demonstrations to the EU Commission and German Cabinet on the risks and opportunities of synthetic media; an ECCV face-tracking tutorial; a Eurographics state-of-the-art report on facial performance capture.
- 2016-2017: Face2Face real-time reenactment demos at SIGGRAPH and Nvidia's GPU Technology Conference; a live demonstration on the Jimmy Kimmel Show; FaceVR, an interactive head-reconstruction demo.

Associated venues: SIGGRAPH, CVPR, ECCV, Eurographics, 3DV, the EU Commission, the German Cabinet, Nvidia GTC.
