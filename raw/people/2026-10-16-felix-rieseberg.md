## felixrieseberg.com

Header: "Hi, I'm Felix. I work on Claude at Anthropic. Electron co-maintainer. Maintainer of too many little open source projects."

The site is built as an interactive 3D-room experience, with a static fallback for devices/browsers where the interactive version might perform badly. The static page says: "Personal websites and portfolios are some of the last places where the Internet is still creative and fun, so I worked very hard on an interactive experience."

- **Anthropic** (2025–now): "I work on whatever allows me to make people productive with AI. In my time at the company so far, I've led engineering for Claude.ai, Cowork, and Code Desktop." claude.com/download
- **Notion** (2023–2025): Notion's desktop apps for Mac and Windows, plus the web plumbing underneath them. notion.com/desktop
- **Stripe** (2021–2023): Two years on Stripe's core payments APIs.
- **Slack** (2016–2021): Nearly five years on Slack's desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux; first writing code, later managing the team.
- **Microsoft** (2011–2016): Five years at Microsoft, evangelizing Windows Phone in Munich and San Francisco, then open source, mostly making `npm install` work on Windows (windows-build-tools).

### Projects

- **Electron** (2016–now): has helped maintain Electron since 2016. electronjs.org
- **Electron Fiddle** (2018–now): a quick way to try Electron — small experiments, any version, no setup. electronjs.org/fiddle
- **Clippy** (2025–now): Clippy from Office 97, back on your desktop and running a language model on your own computer.
- **Language Model Builder** (2026–now): a free Mac app that explains how language models work, then lets you train a small one yourself. languagemodelbuilder.com
- **windows95** (2018–now): Windows 95 in an Electron app, with the hard emulation work done by v86.
- **macintosh.js** (2020): Mac OS 8 in an Electron app pretending to be a 1991 Macintosh Quadra, with the emulation done by Basilisk II.
