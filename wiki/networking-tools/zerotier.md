## Overview

ZeroTier is a software-defined networking platform providing secure connectivity across devices, marketed as "next-generation connectivity and cybersecurity" and scaled through five pricing tiers from free personal use to custom enterprise/defense-grade deployments.

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Devices | Networks | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal | Free | 10 | 1 | AES-256 encryption, perpetual free |
| Essential | $18/mo | 10 (+$2/device) | 10 | OIDC support, basic admin |
| Scale | $179/mo | 100 (+$1.80/device) | Unlimited | Built for service providers |
| Enterprise | Custom | 500+ | — | Self-hosted/compliance options, priority support |
| Quantum | Custom | — | — | ML-KEM-1024 encryption, sovereign deployment |

## Features and Compliance

Feature depth scales with tier: network administration, premium relay support, access control/policy enforcement, API/CLI/UI interfaces, and compliance certifications (SOC 2, GDPR, ISO 27001, FIPS 140-3) concentrated in the higher tiers. Discounts up to 50% are available for MSPs, educational institutions, and charities.

## Target Use Cases

Personal/hobbyist and smart-home networking at the free tier, SMB VPN/SD-WAN at Essential, large multi-tenant deployments at Scale, regulated enterprises at Enterprise, and defense/government/mission-critical infrastructure at Quantum.
