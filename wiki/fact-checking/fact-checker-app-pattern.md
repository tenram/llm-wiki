## Overview

The ExplainX listing describes a systematic fact-checker skill from `shubhamsaboo/awesome-llm-apps`. Its workflow identifies a claim, determines the evidence required, evaluates sources, rates the claim, and supplies context.

## Rating And Evidence Model

The skill uses six ratings: TRUE, MOSTLY TRUE, MIXED, MOSTLY FALSE, FALSE, and UNVERIFIABLE. Its credibility hierarchy places peer-reviewed studies and official statistics above reputable news, expert statements, general news, and social media or blogs.

It highlights statistical cherry-picking, context removal, false equivalences, and logical fallacies. The listing is an ExplainX export and should be attributed to ExplainX and its canonical URL when reused.
