## Overview

GBrain is a team memory and automation product that puts shared memories, skills, and connected accounts into the AI tools a team already uses. It presents memory as plain files organized by people, companies, meetings, projects, ideas, concepts, originals, and sources, with semantic search and separate personal and team workspaces.

## Operating Model

A workspace connects accounts once and assigns each AI a permission level. The product describes connectors for Gmail, calendars, Drive, Stripe, GitHub, Linear, CRM systems, Airtable, and other services, plus an MCP address for additional tools. Skills can run manually or on a schedule; examples include customer communication drafts, launch workflows, churn analysis, email-to-memory, inbox cleanup, and sourced person briefs.

## Trust Boundary

GBrain emphasizes that the team owns what it learns and that connected credentials are held by the service while an AI receives results. The page shows permission levels, daily limits, activity logs, expiring access, and approval behavior for communications. These are product claims from the marketing site; security, retention, export, and authorization details should be verified in the trust and privacy documentation before sensitive use.
