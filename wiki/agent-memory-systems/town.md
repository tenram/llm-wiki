## Overview

Town is a commercial personal AI assistant product. Each user is paired with a customizable "Townie" that learns their work patterns from day one, without manual training, and takes over recurring administrative work: inbox organization, meeting scheduling, task completion, and information synthesis.

## Features

- **Learning and personalization**: adapts to individual communication style and client preferences from the outset.
- **To-Do List Management**: processes task lists and reports status.
- **Routines**: handles recurring work such as email management, meeting prep, competitive research, and relationship tracking.
- **Suggestions**: proactively surfaces optimization opportunities.
- **Team integration**: Townies work across shared team tools and workflows, not just individual accounts.
- **50+ integrations**: Gmail, Google Workspace, Slack, HubSpot, Notion, Linear, Salesforce, and others.

## Target Audience

Busy professionals (investors, sales leaders, talent managers) and teams seeking workflow automation without hand-written prompts.

## Privacy

Emphasizes data encryption, SOC II Type 2 compliance, and no training on user data unless explicitly opted in.
