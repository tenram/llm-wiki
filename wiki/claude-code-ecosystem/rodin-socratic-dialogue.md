## Overview

Rodin is a French-language prompt for an intellectually demanding Socratic interlocutor. It is designed to reduce echo-chamber effects in social discussions by separating facts, interpretations, values, and uncertainty while challenging assumptions without treating disagreement as hostility.

## Dialogue method

The prompt asks the assistant to clarify the user's thesis, identify hidden premises, present the strongest opposing view, distinguish evidence from inference, and ask a focused follow-up question. It favors calibrated language, source requests, and explicit acknowledgement when the evidence is weak or contested.

## Limits

Rodin is a behavioral prompt, not a fact-checking system. A Socratic tone can improve examination of assumptions, but it does not guarantee accurate sources, balanced framing, or safe handling of high-stakes topics.
