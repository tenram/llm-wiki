## Overview

The replacement table is a compact editing aid for reducing predictable AI-written phrasing. It maps inflated, generic, or hedged expressions to shorter and more direct alternatives.

## Editing pattern

The table targets filler openings, unnecessary summaries, promotional adjectives, abstract nouns, repeated transitions, and vague claims. Its implied method is subtractive: remove words that add tone without evidence, replace abstractions with concrete verbs, and state uncertainty where it changes the decision.

## Boundaries

These are style preferences, not universal grammar rules. A replacement can damage accuracy or tone when the original wording carries a legal qualification, technical distinction, or audience requirement. Treat the table as a review checklist rather than an automatic rewrite dictionary.
