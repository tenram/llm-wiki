# Agent Infrastructure as the Product Layer

> Sources: Dor Josef, 2026-10-03
> Raw: [Agent infrastructure week](../../raw/ai-agent-harnesses/2026-11-21-agent-infrastructure-week.md)

## Overview

Dor Josef’s briefing argues that the important agent trend is shifting from frontier models to the infrastructure around them: memory, orchestration, management, observation, and accountability. It connects examples such as Hindsight, Paperclip, Google AX, Codebase Memory MCP, design-canvas agents, and parallel coding-agent tools.

## Main Thesis

The briefing treats agent infrastructure as the product layer that makes agents useful in organizations. Memory systems compound findings, management systems coordinate work and budgets, and observability surfaces make agent decisions visible. Its accountability framing is that an agent’s permissions and goals are set by people and operators, so responsibility cannot be delegated to a vague notion of a “rogue” agent.

## Evidence And Limits

This is a dated trend briefing that cites public rankings, product launches, and commentary. The cited star velocity, valuations, and legal or policy references are source-attributed observations, not independently verified measurements here. Use it as a map of themes and links, not as a durable benchmark.
